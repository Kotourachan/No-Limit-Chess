No-limit Chess

Chess where every capture is a poker hand.

A simple AI for chess and poker. Handy for testing.

Handy for trying out the rules alone. Both players' cards are visible.

How it works
  • Normal chess until someone captures (the king just captures normally, no hand). A capture starts a heads-up poker mini-match.
  • Each side starts the mini-match with 3 BB plus their piece's value, and the player who captures gets +2 BB for initiating. The defender gets -2 BB. A bishop (3) taking a pawn (1) is 8 BB against 2 BB. Values: pawn 1, knight 3, bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9. On the table one chip is one BB.
  • Blinds are 0.5 / 1 BB. The capturer is the dealer for the whole exchange. Hands are played until one player is out of chips.
  • Win the mini-match and the capture stands. Lose it and your capturing piece is lost (the defender stays put) and your move is spent. Recapturing on the same square works the same way, with the recapturer as the capturer.
  • The capturer must show their cards if called. The other player can muck a losing hand.
  • Every decision has a 20 second clock. When it runs out you use your 30 second overtime bank (a fresh one every exchange). With both used up you check if it's free, otherwise you fold. After every decision the table pauses for 2 seconds so you can see the move and the chips.
  • No chips carry between captures. Checkmate (or the usual draws) ends the game. Only the king captures for free. If you're in check and capture with another piece, you still have to win the hand; lose it and you stay in check, so the king falls.
